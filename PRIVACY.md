# Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 15, 2026

HartsOfflineToolkit ("the Software") is engineered as a zero-cloud,
strictly offline, local-execution semantic search appliance for Linux
systems.

## 1. Zero Telemetry & Air-Gapped Operation

- Hardcoded Offline Runtime: The Software enforces `HF_HUB_OFFLINE=1` at
  runtime to block outbound telemetry, socket calls, or remote checkpoint
  requests.
- No Data Collection: We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell personal
  information, document contents, search queries, IP addresses, or usage
  analytics.
- 100% Local Processing: All PDF parsing (`pdfplumber`), embedding
  inference (EmbeddingGemma 2), and vector storage (LanceDB) execute
  strictly on your local hardware.

## 2. Website (hartsofflinetoolkit.com)

Our static project page at hartsofflinetoolkit.com is hosted on Cloudflare
Workers without tracking cookies or third-party advertising scripts.
Cloudflare may temporarily process standard edge connection metadata to
protect against DDoS attacks and abuse.
